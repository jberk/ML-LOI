# Data access

## Restricted data (not in this repository)

### RIDOC administrative records
Individual-level pretrial detention records, January 2012 - February 2023,
provided by the Rhode Island Department of Corrections under an approved
research agreement. These data are not ours to redistribute.

Researchers seeking access should submit a request to RIDOC. Requests are
reviewed by the RIDOC Medical Research Advisory Group and require IRB approval
(Brown University Health Prisoner IRB for this study). The corresponding
author, Justin Berk (justin_berk@brown.edu), can advise on the process.

### Jail Data Initiative (JDI)
National daily jail roster data, January 2020 - May 2025, collected by the NYU
Public Safety Lab. Access is by request: https://jaildatainitiative.org/

### Census of Jails, 2019 (ICPSR 38323)
Used for facility-level context. Distributed by ICPSR under terms that
prohibit redistribution; download it directly:
https://www.icpsr.umich.edu/web/NACJD/studies/38323

## Public data used in the analysis

### ZIP-code median household income
American Community Survey 2019 5-year estimates (table B19013) by ZCTA.
https://data.census.gov/

### Social Vulnerability Index
CDC/ATSDR Social Vulnerability Index, 2018, county level.
https://www.atsdr.cdc.gov/place-health/php/svi/
